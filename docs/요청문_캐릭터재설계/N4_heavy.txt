Create ONE character design image.

FORMAT
- A single character, standing straight, full body.
- STRICT SIDE VIEW, facing RIGHT. Not front, not back, not three-quarter.
- The character fills about 90% of the image height, centered.
- Background: FLAT PURE GREEN (#00FF00), completely empty.
- NO ground line, NO shadow, NO floor, NO horizon, NO text, NO border.
- Painterly 2D game art with soft shading. Not flat vector, not photo, not 3D render.

CHARACTER
A LIVING human arcane archer - a spellbow ranger. Not undead, not a ghost,
not a skeleton. A living person under the gear.

The bow is an ENCHANTED weapon: violet-magenta runes are carved along the limbs
of the bow and the bowstring itself glows faintly with arcane light (#e05cff).
That same arcane light shows in three places and nowhere else:
  - a soft glow deep inside the hood where the eyes are
  - thin rune lines on the gear
  - a small wisp of arcane energy around the bow hand
Everything else is dark grey-green: weathered armor, leather and cloth.

The hood is deep and pointed, and the face inside it is IN SHADOW - only the
faint arcane glow is visible. Do not draw a detailed face.
A quiver of arrows on the back, the arrowheads faintly glowing.

*** THE TWO MOST IMPORTANT RULES ***

1. LEGS FULLY VISIBLE.
   Both legs must be completely exposed from the hip down.
   NOTHING may hang below the hip - no cloak, no robe, no coat, no skirt.
   I must be able to see two separate legs with a clear gap between them.
   This is the single most important requirement in this request.

2. THE BOW IS HELD LOWERED.
   The bow hangs down at the side, pointing toward the ground.
   It is NOT raised, NOT drawn, NOT aimed. No arrow is nocked.
   The arms hang relaxed so they could swing freely.

OUTFIT FOR THIS VERSION
A short segmented plate skirt covering ONLY the hip - it ends immediately
below the belt and does not reach the thighs.
Heavy shoulder guards with violet-magenta runes engraved in the metal.
Fully armored thighs and greaves, thick heavy boots.
This is the bulkiest version, but the legs must still read as two separate
armored shapes.

DO NOT DRAW ANY OF THESE
- A long cloak, long robe, floor-length cape, ankle-length coat, or anything
  that covers or hides the legs. This is the most common mistake - avoid it.
- A dress, skirt, or gown.
- Undead features of any kind: skeleton, exposed bones, skull face, rotting
  flesh, zombie skin, wraith or ghost body, floating or transparent legs.
  This character is ALIVE.
- A female figure.
- A scythe, staff, wand, sword, spear, or wings.
- The bow raised, drawn, or aimed.
- Bright saturated purple covering the whole outfit - the violet-magenta arcane
  light belongs ONLY inside the hood, on the bow runes, and on the bow hand.
- Smoke, mist, aura, magic circles, or glow rings around the body. These are
  drawn by code later; if you draw them in they cannot be animated.
- A ground shadow or a line under the feet.
- Multiple characters, extra limbs, cropped body, text, watermark.
