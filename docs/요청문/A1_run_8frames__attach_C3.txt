Draw an 8-frame RUN CYCLE sprite strip for a 2D game character.

The attached image is a COSTUME AND COLOR reference ONLY — use it for the
hood, the tattered cloak, the quiver, the armor, the violet-magenta soul-fire
and the proportions. Its STANDING POSTURE MUST NOT BE COPIED. Every frame of
this strip is a sprinting pose. No frame may look like the character is
standing still.

FRAMES: 8 frames in ONE horizontal row, evenly spaced, left to right.
This is ONE COMPLETE run cycle = TWO full steps (4 frames per step).

=== THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE: THE FEET NEVER LEAVE THE GROUND ===
All 8 frames share ONE horizontal ground level. The lowest foot touches that
same level in every single frame. NEVER draw the character airborne or
hopping. Do not raise the whole body. The top of the head must stay at almost
exactly the same height in all 8 frames — vary it by no more than 3% of the
body height. Running is sold by the LEGS SPREADING, not by bouncing up.

=== WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGES: STRIDE WIDTH ===
The horizontal distance between the two feet must swing between nearly zero
and about HALF THE BODY HEIGHT:

 Frame 1  PASSING — feet almost together, RIGHT knee lifted and driving
          forward, left leg straight under the body.        stride ~0
 Frame 2  REACHING — right leg swings forward, left leg pushing back.
                                                            stride ~30%
 Frame 3  CONTACT — RIGHT foot plants forward, LEFT leg stretched far
          behind, heel up. WIDEST SPLIT of the cycle.        stride ~50%
 Frame 4  PUSH — body passes over the planted right foot, left leg folding
          forward underneath.                                stride ~20%
 Frame 5  PASSING — feet almost together, LEFT knee lifted and driving
          forward, right leg straight under the body.
          This is the MIRROR of frame 1 — opposite leg.       stride ~0
 Frame 6  REACHING — left leg swings forward, right leg pushing back.
                                                            stride ~30%
 Frame 7  CONTACT — LEFT foot plants forward, RIGHT leg stretched far
          behind, heel up. WIDEST SPLIT.                     stride ~50%
 Frame 8  PUSH — body passes over the planted left foot, right leg folding
          forward underneath. Leads cleanly back into frame 1. stride ~20%

Frames 5-8 are frames 1-4 with the LEGS SWAPPED. They must NOT be copies.

=== THE CLOAK MUST NOT HIDE THE LEGS ===
The long tattered cloak is swept BACKWARD AND UPWARD by the wind of running,
streaming out behind the character like a banner. It must CLEAR THE LEGS
COMPLETELY — both legs are fully visible from hip to boot in every frame,
so the stride can be seen. Do NOT let the cloak hang down over the thighs or
shins. The ragged hem trails behind, not below.

=== TORSO AND ARMS MUST MOVE TOO ===
- The torso is PITCHED FORWARD in every frame, leaning into the run. The
  head is clearly AHEAD of the hips, by roughly 10% of the body height.
- Both arms are BENT about 90 degrees at the elbow and PUMP back and forth,
  OPPOSITE to the legs: when the right leg is forward, the LEFT arm is
  forward. The arms swing visibly — this is a main signal of running.
- The bow is gripped in one hand and held LOW, angled DOWN and BACK so it
  does not block the body. It swings with that arm. Do NOT hold the bow
  raised or aimed forward — this is running, not shooting.
- The shoulders rotate slightly with the arm swing.

Every one of the 8 frames must be clearly DIFFERENT from every other frame,
in the legs AND in the arms AND in the torso angle.

=== GLOW ===
Violet-magenta soul-fire #e05cff, in exactly these places:
  - the two eyes inside the hood
  - one hand / forearm wrapped in drifting cold flame (it pumps with the arm)
  - the bow grip and the bowstring
  - THE TORN HEM OF THE CLOAK — the ragged trailing edge glows clearly and
    streaks backward. REQUIRED, plainly visible.
  NO smoke, NO rising wisps, NO aura around the body. NO glowing chest sigil.

=== FRAME ALIGNMENT ===
- Same body height and same distance from camera in all 8 frames. No zoom.
- The single shared ground level is identical in all 8 frames.
- Each character stays HORIZONTALLY CENTERED inside its own frame. The
  character must NOT travel across the strip.

VIEW: side view, facing RIGHT, in all 8 frames.

STYLE: hand-painted 16-bit game art. Hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no
gradients, no soft blur. Light from the UPPER LEFT. Use a SMALL number of
brightness steps (5 or 6) pushed FAR APART — this is displayed at only
64 pixels tall, so soft mid-tones disappear.

DO NOT DRAW: no outline stroke, no rim light, no glow ring, no ground shadow,
NO GROUND LINE OR FLOOR (do not draw a horizon line at the bottom of the
image), no motion-blur streaks, no speed lines, no dust clouds, no text,
no watermark.

FRAME SPACING — critical for slicing:
Leave a clear EMPTY GAP of at least 40 pixels of pure background between
every pair of frames, and at the left and right edges of the image.
NOTHING may cross into a neighbouring frame — not the bow, not the cloak,
not a wisp of flame. Each frame must be a fully separated island of pixels
surrounded by background on both sides.

BACKGROUND: solid pure GREEN #00FF00, completely flat, nothing else.

Output 4096x512.
