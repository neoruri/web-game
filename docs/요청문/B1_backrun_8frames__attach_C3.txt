Draw an 8-frame RUN CYCLE sprite strip for a 2D game character, seen FROM
BEHIND.

The attached image is a COSTUME AND COLOR reference ONLY — use it for the
hood, the tattered cloak, the quiver, the armor, the violet-magenta soul-fire
and the proportions. Its STANDING POSTURE MUST NOT BE COPIED. Every frame of
this strip is a sprinting pose. No frame may look like the character is
standing still.

=== REAR VIEW ===
The character is seen FROM BEHIND, running AWAY from the viewer, into the
scene. We see the BACK of the hood, the BACK of the cloak, and the quiver
from behind. The face is NOT visible. This must read instantly as "running
away from the camera". As each foot kicks up behind, its SOLE is visible —
that is what sells running away.

FRAMES: 8 frames in ONE horizontal row, evenly spaced, left to right.
ONE COMPLETE run cycle = TWO full steps (4 frames per step).

=== THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE: THE FEET NEVER LEAVE THE GROUND ===
All 8 frames share ONE horizontal ground level. The lowest foot touches that
same level in every single frame. NEVER draw the character airborne or
hopping. The top of the head stays at almost exactly the same height in all
8 frames — vary it by no more than 3% of the body height.

=== WHAT CHANGES: THE LEGS ===
Seen from behind, the legs cross and separate. One foot is planted while the
other kicks up behind, showing its sole.

 Frame 1  PASSING — feet close together, RIGHT knee driving forward,
          left leg straight under the body.
 Frame 2  REACHING — right leg forward, LEFT foot kicking up behind,
          sole starting to show.
 Frame 3  CONTACT — right foot planted, LEFT foot kicked HIGHEST behind,
          sole clearly visible. Widest separation.
 Frame 4  PUSH — body over the planted right foot, left leg folding forward.
 Frame 5  PASSING — feet close together, LEFT knee driving forward.
          MIRROR of frame 1 — opposite leg.
 Frame 6  REACHING — left leg forward, RIGHT foot kicking up behind.
 Frame 7  CONTACT — left foot planted, RIGHT foot kicked HIGHEST behind,
          sole clearly visible.
 Frame 8  PUSH — body over the planted left foot, right leg folding forward.
          Leads cleanly back into frame 1.

Frames 5-8 are frames 1-4 with the LEGS SWAPPED. They must NOT be copies.

=== THE CLOAK MUST NOT HIDE THE LEGS ===
The long tattered cloak is swept UPWARD AND OUTWARD by the wind of running,
lifting away from the legs and streaming toward the viewer. It must CLEAR THE
LEGS COMPLETELY — both legs are visible from hip to boot in every frame. Do
NOT let the cloak hang down over the thighs or shins.

=== TORSO AND ARMS MUST MOVE TOO ===
- The torso is PITCHED FORWARD, leaning away from the viewer into the run.
- Both arms are BENT about 90 degrees and PUMP, OPPOSITE to the legs. Seen
  from behind, the elbows swing out and back visibly.
- The bow is gripped in one hand and held LOW, angled DOWN and BACK.
- The shoulders rotate slightly with the arm swing.

Every one of the 8 frames must be clearly DIFFERENT from every other frame,
in the legs AND in the arms AND in the torso angle.

=== GLOW ===
Violet-magenta soul-fire #e05cff:
  - a faint magenta rim leaking around the edges of the hood (the eyes are on
    the far side, so only a hint of light escapes)
  - the flaming hand, visible pumping at the side
  - the bow grip
  - THE TORN HEM OF THE CLOAK — the ragged trailing edge glows clearly.
    REQUIRED, plainly visible.
  NO smoke, NO rising wisps, NO aura. NO glowing chest sigil.

=== FRAME ALIGNMENT ===
- Same body height and same distance from camera in all 8 frames. No zoom.
- The single shared ground level is identical in all 8 frames.
- Each character stays HORIZONTALLY CENTERED inside its own frame.

STYLE: hand-painted 16-bit game art. Hard edges, no anti-aliasing, no
gradients, no soft blur. Light from the UPPER LEFT. Use a SMALL number of
brightness steps (5 or 6) pushed FAR APART — displayed at only 64 pixels
tall, so soft mid-tones disappear.

DO NOT DRAW: no outline stroke, no rim light, no glow ring, no ground shadow,
NO GROUND LINE OR FLOOR (do not draw a horizon line at the bottom of the
image), no speed lines, no dust clouds, no text, no watermark.

FRAME SPACING — critical for slicing:
Leave a clear EMPTY GAP of at least 40 pixels of pure background between
every pair of frames, and at the left and right edges of the image.
NOTHING may cross into a neighbouring frame. Each frame must be a fully
separated island of pixels surrounded by background on both sides.

BACKGROUND: solid pure GREEN #00FF00, completely flat, nothing else.

Output 4096x512.
