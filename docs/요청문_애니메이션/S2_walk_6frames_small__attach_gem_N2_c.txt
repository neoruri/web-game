ATTACH: gem_N2_c.png  (the reference character)

Draw ONE image that is a WALK CYCLE SPRITE SHEET: 6 small figures in a single
horizontal row.

*** THIS IS A WALK, NOT A RUN ***
The most important difference: in a walk, AT LEAST ONE FOOT IS ALWAYS TOUCHING
THE GROUND. There is no moment where both feet are in the air. The character
never jumps, never floats, never hops.

*** DRAW THE FIGURES SMALL ***
- Each figure is about 1/9 of the image width and about 1/3 of the image height.
  They are SMALL. Do not fill the canvas with big characters.
- All 6 must fit in one row with clear empty space between them.
- Leave at least 50 pixels of EMPTY GREEN between figures - a clean vertical
  strip of pure background crossing the whole image height, with nothing in it.
  No arm, no leg, no cloth may cross into a gap.
- A previous attempt drew them too large, so they overlapped each other.
  Drawing them small is what prevents that.

The image will be scaled down to a tiny game sprite, so fine detail is not
needed. Clear silhouettes and clear foot positions matter far more.

THE CHARACTER
Use the character in the attached image. Same hood, same armor, same quiver,
same dark grey-green colors, same violet-magenta rune glow (#e05cff) on the
shoulder, bracers, belt, greaves and inside the hood.
The same person in all 6 frames.

*** NO BOW ***
In the reference the bow is a separate object beside the character.
Do NOT draw the bow. His hands are EMPTY, closed into loose fists, so the
arms can swing freely.

FORMAT
- STRICT SIDE VIEW, facing RIGHT, in all 6 frames.
- Background: FLAT PURE GREEN (#00FF00), completely empty.
- NO ground line, NO shadow, NO floor, NO horizon, NO text, NO frame borders,
  NO numbers, NO grid lines.
- All 6 figures are the SAME SIZE and stand on the SAME invisible ground line.
- Painterly 2D game art.

*** THE WALK CYCLE - FOLLOW THESE FOOT POSITIONS ***
"Stride" = the horizontal distance between the two feet, as a percentage of
the character's body height.

  frame 1   stride 30%   CONTACT, right foot forward
            *** BOTH FEET FLAT ON THE GROUND ***
            The front heel has just landed and the whole front sole is down.
            The back foot is still flat behind. Both soles touch the ground.

  frame 2   stride  8%   PASSING
            The right leg is straight and vertical, its sole FLAT on the ground,
            carrying all the weight. The left leg swings forward beside it, and
            the left toe SKIMS the ground - lifted no more than 4% of body
            height. It nearly brushes the floor.

  frame 3   stride 18%   PUSH
            The right foot is still on the ground behind, heel lifting but the
            TOE STILL TOUCHING. The left leg reaches forward, heel about to land.

  frame 4   stride 30%   CONTACT, left foot forward
            *** BOTH FEET FLAT ON THE GROUND *** - mirror of frame 1.

  frame 5   stride  8%   PASSING - mirror of frame 2.
            Left leg vertical and flat, right toe skims the ground.

  frame 6   stride 18%   PUSH - mirror of frame 3.
            Left toe still touching behind, right leg reaching forward.

The rhythm is 30 -> 8 -> 18 -> 30 -> 8 -> 18.

*** DO NOT MAKE THE STRIDE BIGGER THAN 30% ***
A previous attempt drew 83%. The legs were split so far apart it looked like a
lunge or a jump, not walking. Keep the feet close. This is a calm, grounded walk.

*** THE FEET MUST LOOK PLANTED ***
- In frames 1 and 4 BOTH soles are flat on the ground.
- In frames 2, 3, 5 and 6 at least one foot is FLAT on the ground and the
  other one is touching or skimming it.
- No frame has both feet in the air.
- The lifted foot never rises more than 4% of body height.

ARMS
- Hanging fairly relaxed with a slight bend, swinging gently OPPOSITE to the legs.
- The swing is SMALL - this is walking, not sprinting. Do not pump the arms
  up and down like a runner.

UPPER BODY
- The torso is nearly upright, leaning forward only very slightly.
- The head rises and falls by no more than 2% of body height across the cycle.
- The body is lowest in frames 1 and 4 (weight landing) and highest in
  frames 3 and 6 (pushing off). Keep this bob tiny.

DO NOT DRAW
- The bow. Hands stay empty.
- Large figures. They must be small enough that 6 fit in a row with wide gaps.
- Figures touching or overlapping each other.
- A running or sprinting pose. Wide splits, high knees, both feet airborne.
- A long cloak covering the legs. The legs stay visible in every frame.
- A ground line, shadow, or horizon.
- Smoke, mist, aura, or glow rings around the body.
- Front, back, or three-quarter view.
- Text, frame numbers, borders, watermark.
