THE POSE FOR THIS IMAGE - FRAME 2 of 6: MID-STRIDE, WEIGHT ON THE RIGHT LEG

a stride of only 10% of body height. Next to the other frames (43%) that reads
as the character STOPPING and then lurching forward. It looks like hopping on
one knee, not running.

*** MAKE THE STRIDE MEDIUM, NOT TINY ***
- The horizontal gap between the two feet is about 26% of body height.
  Not 10%. The legs are clearly apart, just less than in the wide contact pose.
- The LEFT leg is the swinging leg, moving FORWARD past the right leg.
- Its knee is bent and raised to about mid-thigh height, and its foot is lifted
  a little off the ground - about 8% of body height. Visible gap under the boot,
  but not a high knee lift.
- The RIGHT leg is the standing leg.
- The other leg is under the body carrying the weight, foot on the ground,
  knee slightly bent.

The point of this frame is to sit BETWEEN the wide stride and the closed stride.
It should look like the middle of a stride, not a pause.
