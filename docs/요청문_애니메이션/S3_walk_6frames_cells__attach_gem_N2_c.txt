Create ONE image: a 6-frame WALK CYCLE SPRITE SHEET.
Use the attached reference character as the design source.

Same hood, same armor, same quiver on the back, same dark grey-green palette,
same localized violet-magenta rune glow (#e05cff) on shoulder, bracers, belt,
greaves, and inside the hood.

DO NOT DRAW THE BOW. The quiver is allowed, the bow is forbidden.
Both hands are empty, closed into loose relaxed fists.

CANVAS AND LAYOUT
- A WIDE image, roughly 3 times wider than it is tall.
- Flat pure green background: #00FF00.
- Divide the image into SIX EQUAL INVISIBLE CELLS side by side.
- Exactly ONE small figure centred in each cell. Six figures total.
- Each figure is only about ONE THIRD of the image height. They are SMALL.
- Wide empty green space between figures. No character part may cross into
  the space between cells.
- A previous attempt drew the figures large, so they overlapped each other and
  could not be cut apart. Drawing them small inside equal cells prevents that.
- No visible grid, no borders, no frame numbers, no text.

STYLE
- Clean readable 2D game sprite, simplified painterly shading, clear silhouette.
- HARD CLEAN EDGES with no green spill or green fringe around the character.
- The image will be scaled down to a tiny game sprite. Clear silhouettes and
  clear foot positions matter far more than fine detail.
- No shadow, no ground line, no floor, no horizon, no aura, no smoke, no glow ring.

VIEW
- Strict side view, facing RIGHT in all 6 frames.
- Same character, same size, same costume, same proportions in every frame.
- All feet align to the SAME invisible ground line.

*** THIS IS A WALK, NOT A RUN ***
At least one foot ALWAYS touches the ground. There is never a moment with both
feet in the air. No jumping, no floating, no hopping.
The stride stays small and calm.

FRAME POSES
"Stride" = horizontal distance between the two feet, as a % of body height.

  Frame 1  CONTACT, right foot forward, stride 30%.
           BOTH FEET FLAT ON THE GROUND. Front sole flat, back sole flat.
  Frame 2  PASSING, stride 8%.
           Right leg vertical, right sole FLAT on the ground carrying weight.
           Left leg swings forward, left toe SKIMS the ground.
  Frame 3  PUSH, stride 18%.
           Right foot behind, heel lifted, RIGHT TOE STILL TOUCHING the ground.
           Left leg reaches forward, left heel about to land.
  Frame 4  CONTACT, left foot forward, stride 30%. Mirror of frame 1.
           BOTH FEET FLAT ON THE GROUND.
  Frame 5  PASSING, stride 8%. Mirror of frame 2.
           Left sole flat and weight-bearing, right toe skims the ground.
  Frame 6  PUSH, stride 18%. Mirror of frame 3.
           Left toe still touching behind, right leg reaching forward.

The rhythm is 30 -> 8 -> 18 -> 30 -> 8 -> 18.

*** DO NOT MAKE THE STRIDE BIGGER THAN 30% ***
A previous attempt drew 83%. The legs were split so far apart that it looked
like a lunge or a jump, not walking. Keep the feet close together.

*** THE FEET MUST LOOK PLANTED ***
- Frames 1 and 4: BOTH soles flat on the ground.
- Frames 2, 3, 5, 6: at least one foot FLAT on the ground, the other touching
  or skimming it.
- The lifted foot NEVER rises more than 4% of body height.
- No frame has both feet in the air.
- A previous attempt had the feet floating, which made the character look like
  it was trembling in the air instead of walking.

ARMS
- Relaxed, slightly bent, small swing opposite to the legs.
- Do NOT pump the arms up and down like a runner.

BODY
- Torso nearly upright, only a very slight forward lean.
- Head bob no more than 2% of body height across the whole cycle.
- Body lowest in frames 1 and 4, highest in frames 3 and 6, but keep it subtle.

DO NOT DRAW
- The bow. Hands stay empty.
- Large figures. Six must fit in a row with wide gaps.
- Figures touching or overlapping each other.
- A running or sprinting pose: wide splits, high knees, both feet airborne.
- A long cloak covering the legs. The legs stay visible in every frame.
- A ground line, shadow, or horizon.
- Smoke, mist, aura, or glow rings around the body.
- Front, back, or three-quarter view.
- Text, frame numbers, borders, watermark.
