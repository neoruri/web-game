Draw ONE image that is a RUN CYCLE SPRITE SHEET: 8 small figures in a single

*** DRAW THE FIGURES SMALL ***
This is the most important instruction.
- Each figure is only about 1/12 of the image width and about 1/3 of the image
  height. They are SMALL. Do not fill the canvas with big characters.
- All 8 must fit in one row with clear empty space between them.
- Leave at least 40 pixels of EMPTY GREEN between figures - a clean vertical
  strip of pure background crossing the whole image height, with nothing in it.
  No arm, no leg, no cloth may cross into a gap.
- A previous attempt drew them too large, so only 6 fit and they overlapped
  each other. Drawing them small is what prevents that.

The image will be scaled down for a small game sprite, so fine detail is not
needed. Clear silhouettes and clear leg positions matter far more than detail.

THE CHARACTER
Use the character in the attached image. Same hood, same armor, same quiver,
same dark grey-green colors, same violet-magenta rune glow (#e05cff) on the
shoulder, bracers, belt, greaves and inside the hood.
The same person in all 8 frames.

*** NO BOW ***
In the reference the bow is a separate object beside the character.
Do NOT draw the bow. His hands are EMPTY, closed into loose fists, so the
arms can swing freely.

FORMAT
- STRICT SIDE VIEW, facing RIGHT, in all 8 frames.
- Background: FLAT PURE GREEN (#00FF00), completely empty.
- NO ground line, NO shadow, NO floor, NO horizon, NO text, NO frame borders,
  NO numbers, NO grid lines.
- All 8 figures are the SAME SIZE and stand on the SAME invisible ground line.
- Painterly 2D game art.

*** THE RUN CYCLE - FOLLOW THESE STRIDE NUMBERS ***
"Stride" = the horizontal distance between the two feet, as a percentage of the
character's body height. This is what makes it read as running.

  frame 1   stride 45%   CONTACT, right heel striking, left leg stretched back
  frame 2   stride  8%   PASSING, feet nearly together, left knee swinging
                         forward past the right leg, left foot just off the ground
  frame 3   stride 28%   PUSH, right foot pushing off behind, left knee driving
                         forward
  frame 4   stride 45%   CONTACT, left heel striking, right leg stretched back
  frame 5   stride  8%   PASSING, mirror of frame 2
  frame 6   stride 28%   PUSH, mirror of frame 3
  frame 7   stride 45%   same as frame 1
  frame 8   stride  8%   same as frame 2

The rhythm is 45 -> 8 -> 28 -> 45 -> 8 -> 28 -> 45 -> 8.
It goes WIDE, then CLOSED, then MEDIUM, and repeats. That rise and fall is
what makes it look like running.

DO NOT make frame 1 or 4 wider than 45%. A previous attempt drew 83% - the legs
were split so far apart it looked like a lunge or a jump, not a run.

ARMS
- Bent about 90 degrees at the elbow, swinging OPPOSITE to the legs.
- When the right leg is forward, the left arm is forward.
- The arm angle must visibly change from frame to frame.

UPPER BODY
- The torso leans FORWARD in every frame - the head sits about 10% of body
  height ahead of the hips.
- The head rises and falls by no more than 3% of body height across the cycle.
- At least one foot is at or very near the ground in every frame.
  He is RUNNING, not jumping. Do not lift him off the ground.

DO NOT DRAW
- The bow. Hands stay empty.
- Large figures. They must be small enough that 8 fit in a row with gaps.
- Figures touching or overlapping each other.
- A long cloak covering the legs. The legs stay visible in every frame.
- A ground line, shadow, or horizon.
- Smoke, mist, aura, or glow rings around the body.
- Front, back, or three-quarter view.
- Text, frame numbers, borders, watermark.
