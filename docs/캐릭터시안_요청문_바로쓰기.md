## ① 본안 — 자마젠타 (권장)

색조 291도. `main.js` 에서 280~339도가 통째로 비어 있고, 회녹색 바닥(G−R +5.9)의
보색이라 대비가 최대다. 레퍼런스 비교 시험에서도 자마젠타 쪽이 가장 잘 읽혔다.

```
A character design sheet for a 2D dark-fantasy dungeon game.

CHARACTER: a hooded undead archer. Long deep hood that hides most of the
face — only two glowing eyes are visible inside it. A long tattered cloak
with a ragged, shredded hem. Dark worn leather armor with straps across the
chest. A QUIVER OF ARROWS on the back, with the arrow fletching sticking up
above the shoulder. Carries a LARGE RECURVE BOW.

GLOWING ACCENTS — this is the point of the design:
Ghostly violet-magenta soul-fire in the color #e05cff, glowing from exactly
these places and nowhere else:
  1. the two eyes inside the hood (small, sharp, the brightest points)
  2. one hand / forearm wrapped in drifting flame
  3. the bow — the grip and the string glowing as if the fire runs through it
  4. a faint trace along the torn hem of the cloak
  5. SMOKE AURA — thin wisps of glowing violet-magenta smoke rising from the
     shoulders, upper back and the top of the hood, drifting UPWARD and
     fading out. This is the signature of the character.
     The smoke must stay CLOSE to the body — a narrow halo hugging the upper
     body, not a big cloud. It must NOT swallow the silhouette: the shape of
     the shoulders, hood and quiver must still read clearly through and
     around it. Thin separate wisps with dark gaps between them, NOT a soft
     fog.
  NO glowing sigil on the chest. The chest stays plain dark armor.
The flame should look like cold fire — wispy, semi-transparent at the tips.
NOT a solid block of color, NOT a lens flare, NOT a soft blurry haze.

CRITICAL 1 — BRIGHTNESS: the body, cloak and armor must NOT be black. They
are DARK GREY-GREEN, clearly lighter than the background, so the whole
silhouette stays readable even where nothing is glowing. Think "charcoal
grey-green in dim light", not "pure black". The glow is an accent on top of
a readable body, not a replacement for it.

CRITICAL 2 — HIGH LOCAL CONTRAST: this sprite will be shown at only about
64 pixels tall, so soft mid-tones disappear. Use a SMALL number of
brightness steps (5 or 6) and push them FAR APART, so neighbouring surfaces
differ sharply. Every fold, strap and armor plate should be a distinct flat
step against the one next to it. Avoid smooth blending; avoid crowding many
similar mid-tones together.

BODY PALETTE (non-glowing parts) — cool desaturated grey-green:
  #262d27 deepest shadow
  #3c443b mid shadow
  #525a4d base cloak and hood
  #6b7364 lit surface
  #8a9280 highlight
NO blue cast, NO brown, NO saturated color anywhere except the soul-fire.

LAYOUT: one large full-body figure in the center, standing, SIDE VIEW
FACING RIGHT, full body from head to boots. Around it, 4 to 6 smaller
detail studies: a hood close-up, a glowing hand, the smoke wisps rising off
a shoulder, the bow grip, and the arrow fletching in the quiver.

STYLE: detailed hand-painted 16-bit game art. Hard edges, no anti-aliasing,
no gradients, no soft blur. Light source from the UPPER LEFT. Grim, damp,
underworld atmosphere.

DO NOT DRAW: no ground shadow, no floor, no pedestal, no outline stroke, no
border, no text, no labels, no watermark, no signature.

BACKGROUND: solid flat neutral dark grey #2a2a2a, nothing else.

Output a single tall image, 768x1152.
```
